# Five-minute portfolio walkthrough

1. **Problem (30 seconds).** A screening alert is a candidate, not a confirmed sanctioned party; cited text is not necessarily valid legal advice.
2. **Case A (2 minutes).** Show the synthetic EUR 125,000 case, economic and voting calculations, archived legal passages, original unsupported claims and reviewer-recorded decisions. Explain why no verified current-law finding is emitted.
3. **Case B (1 minute).** Compare two same-name-score candidates: DOB `CONTRADICTS` versus `MISSING`. Explain why identity remains unresolved.
4. **Engineering evidence (1 minute).** Summarise fixture reproducibility, traceable remediation commits and reported 984/984 pytest pass result. Keep the technical claim separate from legal acceptance.
5. **Limitations (30 seconds).** No production integration, independent legal conclusions or automatic clearance; future development requires authorised regulatory review.

Use only fictional inputs, redacted screenshots and verifiable project evidence. This public portfolio is not a runnable demonstration until audited source components are added from the private repository.
