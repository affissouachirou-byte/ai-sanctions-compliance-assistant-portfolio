## Known limitations

1. An original AI draft contained **two materially unsupported legal propositions**, despite traceable citations.
2. Historical text accuracy does not prove an operative legal provision, effective date, current designation or jurisdictional nexus.
3. Ownership arithmetic is illustrative and cannot substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal ownership/control determination.
4. A synthetic, unverified possible-control covenant did not propagate into downstream reports under the frozen component contract; it was not established as a defect.
5. Reviewer event integrity was tested; authenticated independent legal authority and enterprise segregation of duties were **not** demonstrated.
6. No approved live financial-institution integrations, production security/availability controls or live regulatory-data currency.
7. No demonstrated screening effectiveness uplift, false-positive reduction, p95 operational latency or real-world productivity benefit.
8. Earlier business acceptance exercises used different rubrics: owner-run 16/16 provisional checks and a previous 8/12 rejected expert-style review; neither constitutes independent legal sign-off.

**Permitted portfolio claim:** a technically regression-tested synthetic prototype with conservative, audit-linked human-review safeguards. **Not permitted:** production-ready, validated legal advice, autonomous sanctions determination, proven detection improvement.
